Floraia: period, fertility and pregnancy tracker

Made for Muslim women, in Arabic and English. Floraia predicts your cycle, reminds you only when it matters, and keeps your data on your iPhone.

  • Period, fertile-window and ovulation predictions that learn from your cycles
  • Hijri dates that match the Umm al-Qura calendar, a qada calculator, and ghusl and qada reminders
  • Pregnancy Mode, Kids Mode, a Home Screen widget and an Apple Watch app
  • One payment unlocks Floraia Gold for life, with no subscription
