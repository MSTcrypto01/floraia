Floraia (فلوريا) is a period, fertility and pregnancy tracker made for Muslim women, in Arabic and English. It predicts your cycle, reminds you only when it matters, and respects your faith and your privacy.

PERIOD & CYCLE TRACKING
• Log your period, flow, symptoms and moods in seconds
• A daily check-in for your mood and the symptoms you usually get
• Predictions for your next period, fertile window and ovulation that learn from your own cycles
• Insights that show which symptoms come with each phase of your cycle
• A calendar with Hijri dates that match the Umm al-Qura calendar
• A PCOS check that explains its result and uses your logged cycles
• Settings for every life stage, including perimenopause

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOU
• Period and fertile-window reminders planned two weeks ahead, so they arrive even when you don't open the app
• No more than two a day, quiet hours at night, and every type can be switched off
• Ghusl and qada reminders, if you want them

ISLAMIC TOOLS
• Qada calculator for the Ramadan fasts you need to make up
• Hajj and Umrah tracking with tips for those days
• Religious content is optional: skip it during setup and none appears

PREGNANCY MODE
• Week-by-week progress with your baby's real length and weight
• Due date, trimester milestones and a hospital-bag reminder
• Period alerts pause during pregnancy and the recovery time after it

KIDS MODE
• A gentle, separate experience for girls under 15, with simple words and a star for every day she logs
• Fertility and pregnancy features stay with married adults

WORKS WITH APPLE HEALTH
• Saves your period days, spotting and basal body temperature to the Health app (HealthKit)
• Reads the period days and temperatures you log on Apple Watch or in other apps, and adds nothing to your history without your review
• Connect during setup or from Profile → Apple Health, and turn it off at any time

WIDGET & APPLE WATCH
• Home Screen widget, plus an Apple Watch app and watch-face complication

COMMUNITY
• Ask questions and share experiences anonymously

PRIVACY
• Your cycle data stays on your iPhone, not on our servers, and is included in your iPhone's own iCloud backup when it's on
• No account needed
• Lock the app with a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID
• Delete all your data at any time
• Apple Health data is never used for advertising
